Drop your files here with these exact names:
  compare.mp4   (video)   or   compare.jpg / .png / .webp   (still image)

A video wins over an image if both exist.

Tips: H.264 .mp4, 10–20 seconds, 1080p or smaller, under ~8 MB each.
