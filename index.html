About Vikshiv Labs

Vikshiv Labs is run by Vikas Chary Alwala, a VFX artist working at a Tollywood VFX studio in Hyderabad, on the kind of big-screen Telugu films that have to hold up in a packed theatre.

Generating images is easy now. Making a hundred shots feel like one film is not: the same faces, the same light, the same world, cut so people keep watching. That is a film-craft problem, and it is the work Vikas does every day on Tollywood productions.

So every Vikshiv film starts with the camera. Lens, light and framing are decided before anything is generated, every shot is storyboarded and approved, and the finish is done like a real post-production pipeline. Alongside client work, we make our own short films as Vikshiv Labs Originals.
